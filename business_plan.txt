Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Platform

1. Project Overview
This project aims to design and develop a multi-vendor e-commerce platform that enables multiple sellers to offer products through a centralized digital marketplace. The system allows vendors to manage their products and orders while providing customers with a seamless online shopping experience.
The platform is implemented as a modern web application using scalable technologies and integrates third-party services such as payment systems.

2. Problem Statement
In many markets, especially among small and medium-sized businesses:
* Sellers rely heavily on social media platforms (e.g., Facebook, Instagram)
* There is no structured product management system
* Orders are handled manually via messaging
* Lack of payment integration leads to inefficiency
* Scaling operations becomes difficult
As a result, both vendors and customers experience inefficiencies, lack of transparency, and limited growth potential.

3. Proposed Solution
The proposed system is a centralized online marketplace that provides:
For Vendors:
* Easy registration and onboarding
* Product management (create, update, delete)
* Order tracking and management
* Access to a vendor dashboard
For Customers:
* Product browsing and searching
* Shopping cart functionality
* Secure checkout and payment (QPay)
* Order history and tracking
For Administrators:
* Vendor approval system
* User management
* Order monitoring and system oversight

4. Target Users
Customers
* Individuals purchasing products online
* Typically aged 18-40
* Prefer convenient and fast digital shopping
Vendors
* Small and medium-sized businesses
* Independent sellers lacking technical infrastructure
Administrator
* Platform owner responsible for managing the system

5. Value Proposition
The system provides:
* A centralized marketplace for multiple vendors
* Simplified onboarding for sellers
* Structured and efficient order management
* Improved product discovery for customers
* Scalable architecture for future growth
Compared to informal social media selling, the platform offers a more reliable and professional environment.

6. Key Features (MVP Scope)
Customer Features:
* User registration and login
* Product browsing and filtering
* Shopping cart
* Checkout with QPay integration
* Order tracking
Vendor Features:
* Vendor registration
* Product management (CRUD)
* Order management
Admin Features:
* Vendor approval
* User management
* Order monitoring

7. Business Model
The platform can generate revenue through:
* Commission-based model
A percentage fee charged per completed transaction
* Subscription model (future)
Vendors pay a monthly fee for platform usage or premium features

8. Market & Competitor Analysis
Competitors:
   * International: Amazon, Alibaba
   * Local: Existing Mongolian e-commerce platforms
Differentiation:
   * Focus on simplified vendor onboarding
   * Tailored for local payment system (QPay)
   * Designed as a lightweight and scalable solution

9. Feasibility Analysis
Technical Feasibility:
   * Uses modern technologies (Next.js, Supabase, Tailwind)
   * Cloud-based infrastructure ensures scalability
Economic Feasibility:
   * Low initial cost (open-source tools + cloud services)
   * Scalable business model
Operational Feasibility:
   * Easy to use for both vendors and customers
   * Minimal training required

10. Limitations
   * Prototype-level implementation
   * Limited real-world load testing
   * Payment integration may be limited to sandbox/testing environment
   * No mobile application (web-only system)

11. Future Improvements
   * Recommendation system (AI-based product suggestions)
   * Vendor analytics dashboard
   * Mobile application (React Native or Flutter)
   * Advanced search and personalization
   * Multi-language support
